# Home Page Animation — Script & Storyboard

**Working title:** *Big Firm Expertise. Small Firm Heart.*
**Runtime:** ~75 seconds
**Format:** Motion-graphics / kinetic-typography brand summary for the home page hero
**Voice:** Warm, confident, plain-spoken. One narrator (Lesley's voice or a close match).
**Palette / type:** Pull from the site design system — brand navy + accent, logo animation on entry and exit.
**Music:** Understated, building. Soft piano/strings under; lands on a confident final chord at the logo.

---

## SCENE 1 — The Hook (0:00–0:09)

**On screen:** Black. A single cursor-blink of light, then bold kinetic type fades up, one line replacing the next.
> *Big Firm Expertise.*
> *Small Firm Heart.*

**Motion:** Words scale in with weight; the two lines settle side by side, the accent color underlining "Bold."

**VO:**
"Every business deserves a great CFO. Not just the ones big enough to be on a typical firm's radar."

---

## SCENE 2 — The Problem (0:09–0:22)

**On screen:** A business owner silhouette pulled between two arrows — "Run the business" / "Steer the business." A stack of receipts and a spreadsheet dissolve into a question mark.

**Motion:** Numbers scroll fast, then freeze — highlighting that data alone doesn't decide.

**VO:**
"Growth outpaces leadership. Bookkeeping tells you what happened — it can't tell you what to do next. And most owners need senior financial judgment long before they can justify a six-figure hire."

**On-screen text (lower third):** *Growth outpaces leadership capacity.*

---

## SCENE 3 — The Idea: Fractional (0:22–0:34)

**On screen:** A full "CFO" title block splits into a clean fraction — a portion lights up in the accent color. Icon: a dial scaling up and down.

**Motion:** The dial scales with the phrase "the scope you need."

**VO:**
"That's what fractional leadership is for. C-suite expertise, sized to you. Senior-level guidance and hands-on partnership — engaged at the scope and pace your organization needs. No six-figure salary. No junior hand-offs."

**On-screen text:** *Fractional by design.*

---

## SCENE 4 — Four Ways to Engage (0:34–0:50)

**On screen:** Four cards fan out and lock into a 2×2 grid, each with its icon animating in:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fractional CFO** — ongoing leadership | **Projects** — defined engagements |
| **Interim CFO** — leadership continuity | **Start-up & Middle Market** — foundation to scale |

**Motion:** Each card lifts as it's named, then the grid settles.

**VO:**
"Financial leadership, four ways to engage. Ongoing fractional CFO guidance. Defined projects with a clear end date. Interim leadership when a finance chief departs. And support that takes you from your first financial foundation all the way to scale."

---

## SCENE 5 — Why Us / The Proof (0:50–1:03)

**On screen:** Proof stats count up rapidly, one after another:
`30+ Years` · `CPA` · `PwC Alumni` · **`2023 CFO of the Year`** (accent highlight, small award ribbon)

**Motion:** The award ribbon presses in with a subtle shine sweep.

**VO:**
"Behind the work: over thirty years of experience. A licensed CPA and PwC alumni. And Lesley Hazleton — the Wichita Business Journal's 2023 CFO of the Year. Big-firm rigor. Owner-level care. Outcomes you can see — not billable hours."

**On-screen text:** *Direct senior-level access. Every time.*

---

## SCENE 6 — Who We Serve (1:03–1:10)

**On screen:** Three icons slide in together — a storefront, a single person, a hand-heart (nonprofit).

**VO:**
"Built for the businesses others overlook — growing companies, sole proprietors, and the nonprofits doing important work."

---

## SCENE 7 — The Spark (1:10–1:17)

**On screen:** A spark/zap icon ignites; a subtle circuit motif animates behind the words "Make Bold Spark."

**VO:**
"And when the problem needs a technical solution, we build it — with Make Bold Spark, our technical arm."

**On-screen text:** *Make Bold Spark ↗*

---

## SCENE 8 — Close / CTA (1:17–1:15… → 1:25)

**On screen:** Everything clears to brand navy. Logo animates in. Tagline underneath, then the CTA button pulses once.

> **Make Bold Solutions**
> *Bold decisions. Rooted in strategy.*
> **[ Get in Touch ]**

**VO:**
"Make Bold Solutions. Bold decisions, rooted in strategy. The first step is a conversation — no obligation, no pressure."

**Music:** Resolves on final chord as the logo locks.

---

## Production notes

- **Length options:** Full cut ~75s for the hero; a 30s cut = Scenes 1, 3, 4, 8 for social/LinkedIn.
- **Silent-autoplay version:** The on-screen text lines carry the message without VO — home-page heroes usually autoplay muted. Keep captions burned in.
- **Consistency:** Matches the existing `fractional-cfo-intro.mp4` and `projects-intro.mp4` pattern — same logo bookends and type treatment.
- **File target:** `docs/home-intro.mp4` (+ `.webm` and a poster frame), referenced from the home hero like the other intro videos.
